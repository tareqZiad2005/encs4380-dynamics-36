3 · Our design

      

        

          With a PI controller on K/(τs + 1), the closed loop has the characteristic equation
          τs² + (1 + K·Kp)s + K·Ki = 0 (validation check 2). Matching it to s² + 2ζωn s + ωn² gives the gains
          directly, with ωn chosen from the settling time, ts ≈ 4/(ζωn):
        

        
ωn = 4/(ζ·ts) · Ki = τωn²/K · Kp = (2ζωnτ − 1)/K
